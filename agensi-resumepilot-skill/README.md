
# ResumePilot AI – Evidence-Based Resume Evaluator

## Overview

ResumePilot AI is an AI-powered career assistant that
helps job seekers evaluate their resumes against job
descriptions.

## What It Does

- Extracts job requirements.
- Analyzes candidate evidence.
- Compares qualifications with job requirements.
- Identifies missing skills.
- Provides ATS improvement recommendations.
- Generates interview preparation questions.
- Produces a structured evaluation report.

## How to Use

1. Install the skill in a compatible AI agent.
2. Provide your resume or CV.
3. Provide the target job description.
4. Ask the AI to evaluate your application.
5. Review the evidence-based report.
6. Apply the suggested improvements.

## Important Limitations

This skill provides decision-support guidance.
It does not guarantee employment, ATS approval,
or an interview invitation.

Users must verify all recommendations and
ensure that their application information is accurate.
